STEP 1 · QUESTION

Humans are the cause

0 – 10 min · 6.2.4
  • IPCC AR6 (2021): "It is unequivocal that human influence has warmed the atmosphere, ocean and land." Observed warming ~1.1 °C (2011–2020) and ~1.2–1.3 °C by 2023.
  • Evidence: rising GHGs; stratosphere cooling while troposphere warms (signature of GHGs, not the Sun); isotopic signature of fossil carbon; models.

STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Ecosystem impacts

10 – 25 min · 6.2.5
  • Coral bleaching: heat stress expels zooxanthellae; mass bleaching events 1998, 2010, 2016, 2024.
  • Range and biome shifts: poleward and upslope; tree lines rising; tundra greening.
  • Phenology: earlier flowering and migration; mismatches with food supply.
  • Resilience falls with repeated heat, drought, fire; tipping points (Amazon dieback).
✏️ Worked example: Phenology mismatch
  1. Caterpillars now peak 10 days earlier (warmer spring).
  2. Great tits still lay eggs at the same time (day-length cue).
  3. Chicks hatch after food peak.
  4. Fewer chicks survive.

STEP 3 · TEST

Societal impacts

25 – 40 min · 6.2.6
  • Water stress, crop failures and food insecurity, heatwaves and disease (malaria spreading to highlands), sea-level rise and coastal flooding, infrastructure damage, displacement and migration.
  • Impacts depend on exposure (where you live), sensitivity (dependence on climate-sensitive livelihoods) and adaptive capacity (money, technology, institutions).
  • Low-income, rural and coastal communities and small island states are most vulnerable.
