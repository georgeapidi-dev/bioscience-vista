Key facts

EE requirements: IB Extended essay guide, first assessment 2027: maximum 4,000 words; 500-word reflective statement on the RPF; three reflection sessions (first, interim, viva voce); criteria A 6, B 6, C 6, D 8, E 4 (30).

A student must achieve grade D or higher in the EE to be awarded the diploma. The EE is about 40 hours of work with 3–5 hours of supervision.

Two pathways

Pathway What it means Think about
Subject-focused You investigate your idea within the academic boundaries of one DP subject, using its knowledge, concepts, theories, perspectives or methods. Strongly recommended: a subject you already study in the DP, so you can build on what you know.
Interdisciplinary You integrate knowledge, concepts, theories, perspectives or methods from two DP subjects so they work together on your topic. Integration develops as you research; it does not have to be there at the start. Choose from the subjects available for your session.

Cross-disciplinary subjects: environmental systems and societies (ESS) and literature and performance can only be taken on the subject-focused pathway. For a topic that combines geography and biology, consider ESS (subject-focused).

Questions to take your idea for a walk

  • Why am I interested in this topic, and why is it important?

  • Is my topic appropriate for the DP subject(s) I am considering?

  • What possible questions have emerged from my initial reading?

  • Are there any ethical issues to consider at this early stage?

  • What research methods or approaches could I use, and why would they be suitable?
