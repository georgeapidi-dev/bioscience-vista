The viva voce is the 20–30 minute final reflection session after you hand in the final version. It celebrates your essay, reflects on your learning and confirms authenticity.

Questions to prepare (from the guide)

Question My notes
Can I confidently say that my essay is authentic?
Does my essay conform to the IB academic integrity policy?
What skills and understandings have I learned? How could these be transferred to other situations, now and in the future?
What challenges have I experienced and how have these been overcome?
What aspects of the extended essay experience have I enjoyed the most?

Other questions supervisors often ask

  • Talk me through how your research question changed and why.

  • Which source changed your thinking most, and why did you trust it?

  • If you started again, what would you do differently?

  • What does your conclusion not tell us?
