4. Checklist

☐ It is a question ending with "?".

☐ It invites analysis ("To what extent…", "How does…"), not a yes/no answer or a description.

☐ It names the IV with its range and units, and the DV with how it is measured (sciences).

☐ It names the specific organism, system, case or context.

☐ It can be answered with the time, equipment and sources I have.

☐ It is focused enough for 3,000 words (IA) or 4,000 words (EE).

☐ It raises no safety or ethical problems I cannot solve.

5. Weak, improved, strong

Quality Example Why
Weak "Effects of temperature on enzymes" A topic: no organism, no range, no measurement, not a question.
Improved "How does temperature affect the activity of catalase?" A question with an IV and a DV, but no range, method or enzyme source.
Strong "How does temperature (20, 30, 40, 50 and 60 °C) affect the initial rate of oxygen production (cm³ s⁻¹), measured with a gas syringe, by catalase from potato (Solanum tuberosum) at pH 7.0?" IV with range and units, DV with unit and method, enzyme source and a controlled condition.
