Notes
• SD uses the sample formula (n − 1): STDEV. It is most meaningful with 5 or more repeats.
• Record raw values to the precision of the instrument; put units and uncertainties in the headings, not in the cells.
• Do not delete unusual values: investigate them and explain in your report if you exclude any from processing.
• Graph: the means with ± 1 SD error bars. Edit the chart titles to your own variables and units.
