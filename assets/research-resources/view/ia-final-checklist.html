Requirements

IA requirements: IB DP Biology guide, first assessment 2025 (for May/Nov 2027 and 2028): 20% of the grade; 24 marks (research design, data analysis, conclusion, evaluation; 6 each); maximum 3,000 words; same at SL and HL.

Research design

☐ Research question described within a specific and appropriate context (IV with range and units, DV and how it is measured, organism or system).

☐ Background theory of direct relevance, with sources.

☐ Methodological considerations explained: measurement methods, range, interval, repeats, precision, control of variables.

☐ Safety, ethical and environmental issues recognised.

☐ Method detailed enough to be reproduced.

Data analysis

☐ Raw data table(s) with titles, units, uncertainties and consistent decimal places.

☐ Qualitative observations recorded.

☐ Processing shown (means, SD, uncertainties) with a sample calculation.

☐ A suitable statistical test, with the reason for choosing it and the result.

☐ Graph(s) with title, labelled axes with units, error bars and a caption saying what they show.

Conclusion

☐ The research question is answered using the processed numbers and their uncertainties.

☐ The null hypothesis is rejected or not, with the p-value.

☐ Findings are compared with the scientific context, with traceable citations.

☐ No claims of "proof"; a correlation is not presented as a cause.

Evaluation

☐ Specific (not generic) weaknesses and limitations, with their relative impact explained.

☐ Random and systematic errors distinguished.

☐ Realistic improvements linked to each weakness, explained.

☐ Limits to the scope of the conclusion (range, system, assumptions).

Presentation and integrity

☐ Within 3,000 words.

☐ Every source cited in the text and in the reference list, in one consistent style.

☐ Every figure and table numbered and titled.

☐ Any AI use acknowledged as my school and the IB require; all data are my own.

☐ Proof-read; file named as my teacher asked.
