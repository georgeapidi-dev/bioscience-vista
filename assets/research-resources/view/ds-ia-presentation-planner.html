Plan a recorded multimedia presentation of up to 10 minutes: introduction, analysis and evaluation, conclusion.

By George Apidi

Name Class Supervisor Date

Rules for the presentation

Maximum 10 minutes, one file. Sections labelled and in order: Introduction, Analysis and evaluation, Conclusion.

An audible recorded commentary in your own voice throughout (or text-to-speech of your own text). Keep background audio quiet under your voice. All text must be legible; subtitles are allowed.

Cite sources at the point of use with written, visual or spoken cues, and include them in the reference list.

Timing plan

Section Suggested time What it must do My planned time
Introduction 1 minute Re-state the same inquiry focus as the IPD and briefly outline its significance for digital society.
Analysis and evaluation 6–7 minutes Your own sustained, well-supported analysis and evaluation of impacts and implications for people and communities, using evidence.
Conclusion 2 minutes Further insight (your new understanding) and a substantiated discussion of emerging trends and future developments.
