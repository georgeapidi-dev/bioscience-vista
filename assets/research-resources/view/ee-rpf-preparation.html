How criterion E works

Criterion E (4 marks) applies only to the reflective statement on the RPF. Guiding question: "Does the student evaluate the effect of the extended essay learning experience on them as a learner?"

Top band: reflection is consistently evaluative and includes specific examples; it consistently shows growth and transfer of learning.

Maximum 500 words; examiners do not read beyond 500. If the RPF is blank or missing, criterion E is zero.
