STEP 3 · TEST

Part B: measure the mass of five volumes

41 – 58 min · Q17
  • 1. Stand the balance on a flat, dry bench and switch it on.
  • 2. Put the EMPTY measuring cylinder on the balance.
  • 3. Press TARE: the display must read 0.0 g.
  • 4. Take the cylinder off and pour water in to 20 cm³.
  • 5. Read the BOTTOM of the meniscus at eye level. Use the pipette for the last few drops.
  • 6. Dry the outside of the cylinder.
  • 7. Put it back on the balance and write the mass to one decimal place.
  • 8. Do the same volume a second time, then 25, 30, 35 and 40 cm³.
