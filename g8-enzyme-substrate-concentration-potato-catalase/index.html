The puzzle

0 – 5 min · Step 1 · prediction (5 min)
Not marked
Notes

an enzyme is a biological catalyst. The substrate fits into the enzyme's active site like a key in a lock. Catalase breaks hydrogen peroxide down into water and oxygen. More foam in 1 minute means a faster reaction. More enzyme means more active sites; more substrate means the active sites are filled more often, until every one is full.

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Why the two graphs differ

40 – 50 min · Step 4 · Question 3 (10 min)
  • GRAPH A (more enzyme): a straight line going up. Each extra disc adds catalase, so more active sites, so more peroxide is broken down each second. This works because there is plenty of substrate for all the extra active sites.
  • GRAPH B (more substrate): the line goes up at first, then levels off. The flat part is the PLATEAU. At first there are empty active sites; at the plateau every active site is full, so the extra substrate has to wait.
