STEP 3 · TEST

Enzymes are biological catalysts

15 – 30 min · Q3 · explain (15–30 min, video 1)
  • An ENZYME is a protein that speeds up a chemical reaction in a living thing, without being used up itself.
  • A CATALYST speeds up a reaction without being changed or used up. Enzymes are made by living cells, so they are BIOLOGICAL catalysts.
  • Enzymes are reusable: when an enzyme has finished with one substrate, it can do the same job again, thousands of times a second. So a cell needs only a very small amount.

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

The active site and specificity

30 – 45 min · Q4 and Q5 · explain (30–45 min)
  • The ACTIVE SITE is a small dip on the enzyme. It is the only part that does the work, and it has one particular shape.
  • An enzyme is SPECIFIC: its active site has one shape, so it works on one substrate only, as one key opens one lock.
  • Each enzyme works on only one type of substrate, which is why the body needs many different enzymes.
