Your hair is mostly keratin, which is a protein. Your muscles and a growing child are built from the same kind of molecule. What makes it so useful?

By the end of this lesson I will be able to

  • Identify amino acids as the building blocks of proteins.
  • Explain that amino acids join by peptide bonds to form protein chains.
  • State the main functions of proteins.
  • Identify plant and animal sources of protein.
  • Explain why growing people need protein, using Claim, Evidence and Explanation.
The big ideaProteins are long chains of amino acids that fold into shapes. The shape decides the job.
