STEP 3 · TEST

What is reproduction, and why does it matter?

45 – 55 min · Activity 3
  • REPRODUCTION is the biological process by which living things produce new individuals (offspring) of the same species.
  • It is one of the characteristics of all living things (MRS GREN). Every organism alive today exists because its ancestors reproduced.
  • IMPORTANCE: it replaces individuals that die, so the species continues; it passes genes from parents to offspring; and sexual reproduction creates VARIATION, which lets a species adapt and survive change.
  • ASEXUAL reproduction needs one parent and no gametes: the offspring are genetically identical clones. SEXUAL reproduction needs two gametes to fuse: the offspring are genetically varied.

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Mitosis, meiosis and reproduction

55 – 65 min · Activity 3
  • MITOSIS makes two genetically identical cells with the full (diploid) chromosome number. It is how organisms GROW, REPAIR, and REPRODUCE ASEXUALLY.
  • MEIOSIS makes four genetically different cells with HALF the chromosome number (haploid): the GAMETES (sperm and eggs; pollen and ovules).
  • FERTILISATION fuses two haploid gametes into a diploid ZYGOTE, which then grows by MITOSIS into a new organism.
  • So sexual reproduction needs BOTH: meiosis to make the gametes, mitosis to build the new body.
