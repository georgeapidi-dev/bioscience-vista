STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Build the energy chain

about 15 min
  • Monomer: one small unit. Dimer: two units joined. Polymer: many units joined in a chain.
  • glucose → starch / cellulose / glycogen; amino acids → protein; fatty acids + glycerol → lipids
  • Digestive enzymes break polymers into monomers: amylase (starch), protease (protein), lipase (fats).
