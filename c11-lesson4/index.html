STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Inside or outside the cell?

15 min
Memory hook: INtra = INside · EXtra = EXported
Worked example Outline the difference between intracellular and extracellular enzymes, with one example of each. [2]
Model answer
  • Intracellular enzymes catalyse reactions inside the cell, e.g. glycolysis/Krebs cycle enzymes ✓
  • extracellular enzymes are secreted and catalyse reactions outside the cell, e.g. digestive enzymes such as pepsin/amylase in the gut ✓

STEP 3 · TEST

Does metabolism make heat? Test with data

15 min
Memory hook: No transfer is perfect → the lost energy keeps us warm.
Worked example Explain why all metabolising cells release heat, and why this is important for mammals. [3]
Model answer
  • Energy transfers in metabolic reactions (e.g. respiration) are not 100% efficient ✓
  • the energy not transferred to useful products (e.g. ATP) is lost as heat ✓
  • mammals use this heat to maintain a constant body temperature (endothermy) ✓

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Linear and cyclical pathways

20 min
Memory hook: Linear = Line (start → finish) · Cycle = Circle (start comes back)
Worked example Compare and contrast glycolysis and the Krebs cycle as metabolic pathways. [3]
Model answer
  • Both are series of enzyme-catalysed steps / both are part of cell respiration / both oxidise substrates ✓
  • glycolysis is linear (glucose → pyruvate) whereas the Krebs cycle is cyclical (oxaloacetate regenerated) ✓
  • glycolysis occurs in the cytoplasm, the Krebs cycle in the mitochondrial matrix ✓
