STEP 1 · QUESTION

The cholinergic synapse

0 – 8 min · 15.1.7
  • Action potential reaches the presynaptic knob; voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels open; Ca²⁺ enters.
  • Vesicles of acetylcholine (ACh) fuse with the membrane (exocytosis); ACh diffuses across the cleft.
  • ACh binds receptors on the postsynaptic membrane; Na⁺ channels open; depolarisation.
  • Acetylcholinesterase breaks ACh down; products recycled.

STEP 3 · TEST

Neuromuscular junction and muscle structure

20 – 32 min · 15.1.9 – 15.1.10
  • Motor neurone releases ACh onto the sarcolemma; depolarisation spreads down T-tubules.
  • Sarcoplasmic reticulum releases Ca²⁺.
  • Muscle fibre → myofibrils → sarcomeres (Z line to Z line): thin actin and thick myosin filaments; light and dark bands.

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

The sliding filament model

32 – 46 min · 15.1.11
  • Ca²⁺ binds troponin, which moves tropomyosin off the myosin-binding sites on actin.
  • Myosin heads (with ADP + Pi) bind actin: cross-bridges.
  • Power stroke: heads tilt, pulling actin towards the centre; ADP and Pi released.
  • ATP binds; heads detach; ATP hydrolysis re-cocks the heads. Repeat.
  • Sarcomere shortens; I band and H zone shorten; A band stays the same.
✏️ Worked example: Band changes on contraction
  1. Relaxed sarcomere: 2.5 µm; contracted: 2.0 µm.
  2. A band (myosin length) unchanged: 1.6 µm.
  3. I band shortens: actin and myosin overlap more.
  4. H zone shortens or disappears.
