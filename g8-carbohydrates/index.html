Nutrition: carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, vitamins, mineralsCognito · 6:59 · plays here
  • Carbohydrates are one of four main groups of biological molecules, with proteins, lipids and nucleic acids.
  • Every carbohydrate contains carbon, hydrogen and oxygen only. The hydrogen and oxygen are in the same 2:1 ratio as in water.
  • Monosaccharides: one unit (glucose, fructose). Disaccharides: two joined (maltose, sucrose, lactose). Polysaccharides: many joined (starch, glycogen, cellulose).
TypeExamples

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

What carbohydrates actually do

45 – 55 min · Tasks 15–16 · Think like a scientist (10 min)
  • Glucose is used in respiration in every cell to release energy.
  • Plants store spare glucose as starch; animals store it as glycogen in the liver and muscles.
  • Humans cannot digest cellulose, so it is fibre, which keeps the gut working.
