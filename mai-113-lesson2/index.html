AHL 1.13 Adding sinusoidal functions using complex numbers (HL)

IB Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation · 1.13 Polar and Euler forms · Lesson 2 · 1.13.3 · HL only (AHL) · 75 minutes · Prepared by VistaEd
Guiding question: What happens when two alternating voltages are added?

🎯 Start here

Two AC supplies with the same frequency but different phases combine into a single sine wave. Engineers add them as complex numbers (phasors) instead of using trig identities.

STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Adding

10 – 25 min · 1.13.3
  • Add phasors in Cartesian form, convert back.
✏️ Worked example: 3 sin t + 4 cos t
  1. 4 cos t = 4 sin(t + π/2): phasor 4i.
  2. Sum 3 + 4i.
  3. Modulus 5, argument arctan(4/3) = 0.927.
  4. 3 sin t + 4 cos t = 5 sin(t + 0.927).
