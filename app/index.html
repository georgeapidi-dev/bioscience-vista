Get the Learnova app

Install Learnova on your phone, tablet or computer. It opens in its own window from your home screen or desktop, starts faster, and keeps the pages you have visited available without internet. It is free and takes no space in an app store; updates arrive automatically.

🤖 Android phone or tablet

  1. Open biosciencevista.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app above, or tap the ⋮ menu and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. The app appears on your home screen.

🍎 iPhone or iPad

  1. Open biosciencevista.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (□↑).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

💻 Windows, Mac or Chromebook

  1. Open biosciencevista.com in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click Install app above, or the install icon (⊕) at the right of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. Find it in your Start menu, Launchpad or dock.

Use the site offline

After your first visit with internet, the main pages and the science tools open without a connection, and every lesson you open is saved on this device. To save all lessons and notes at once, press the button below (about 20 MB, best on Wi-Fi). Logging in, quizzes, exams, bookings and sending marks to your teacher need the internet; lessons let you download your work instead.
