A typical learning week

  1. Watch
    the lesson video before or after class
  2. Complete
    the interactive lesson or worksheet
  3. Apply
    in a virtual lab, discussion or practical
  4. Review
    your feedback, then try an exam-style question

Consistency is the key to success in IB, IGCSE and A Level science. Check My learning every week for new feedback and messages.

Academic integrity

  • All work you submit must be your own.
  • Plagiarism is not permitted.
  • Use AI tools only as your school and IB academic integrity guidelines allow. The AI feedback here helps you improve; it does not do the work for you.
  • If you are unsure, always ask before submitting work.

About your teacher

George Edwin Apidi

I teach IB Diploma Biology and MYP Science at Aga Khan Academy Mombasa, and I have taught IB, IGCSE and A Level Biology and Chemistry in Kenya and Ethiopia since 2019. I hold a Master of Education in Learning Design and Technology and a Bachelor of Education in Science (Biology and Chemistry with IT).

My teaching focuses on active learning, inquiry and critical thinking: you will learn to understand, analyse, evaluate and apply biological concepts through lab work, data analysis, case studies and technology that makes complex ideas easier to see.
