Routine

A typical learning week

  1. Watch
    the lesson video before or after class
  2. Complete
    the interactive lesson or worksheet
  3. Apply
    in a virtual lab, discussion or practical
  4. Review
    your feedback, then try an exam-style question

Consistency is the key to success in IB, IGCSE and A Level science. Check My learning every week for new feedback and messages.

Academic integrity

  • All work you submit must be your own.
  • Plagiarism is not permitted.
  • Use AI tools only as your school and IB academic integrity guidelines allow. The AI feedback here helps you improve; it does not do the work for you.
  • If you are unsure, always ask before submitting work.

Help and support

  • Ask your teacher a question from My dashboard, or in the forum.
  • Send a message from Messages after you log in.
  • Ask in the class chat.
  • Help is available for content, practical work and IA guidance. Ask early.

About BioscienceVista

BioscienceVista · biosciencevista.com

Created by George Apidi, science teacher and the site's admin.

Interactive science lessons for IB Diploma, MYP, IGCSE and A Level students. Every lesson follows six inquiry steps, gives instant feedback, and lets you learn at your own pace, in a class with your teacher or on your own as an independent learner.
