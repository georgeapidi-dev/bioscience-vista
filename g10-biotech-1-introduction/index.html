STEP 2 · EXPLORE

What is biotechnology?

10 – 25 min · Concept introduction

📺 Watch and learn

Watch before you answer, or afterwards to check your understanding. Plays here, inside the lesson.

Food production: breadMr Exham Biology · 2:39 · plays hereFood production: fermentersMr Exham Biology · 1:59 · plays here
  • BIOTECHNOLOGY: the use of living organisms, their cells or their biological molecules (such as enzymes and DNA) to make useful products or solve problems.
  • It uses CELLULAR processes (fermentation, respiration, cell division, protein synthesis) and BIOMOLECULAR processes (enzyme reactions, copying and cutting DNA).
  • Traditional biotechnology: bread, yoghurt, cheese, beer. Modern biotechnology: genetic engineering, DNA profiling, cloning, stem cell therapy.

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Biotechnology in everyday life

45 – 65 min · Group discussion
  • FOOD: bread, yoghurt, cheese; enzymes in fruit juice; disease-resistant and nutrient-rich crops.
  • MEDICINE: insulin, vaccines, antibiotics, stem cell therapies.
  • ENVIRONMENT: sewage treatment, biofuels, breaking down pollution.
  • FORENSICS: DNA profiling to solve crimes and identify people.
  • BENEFITS: more and safer food, new medicines, cleaner energy, less waste, solving crimes.
Show it your way. Group discussion: where have you met biotechnology in the last week? List at least four examples.
