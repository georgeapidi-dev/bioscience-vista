🧠 ATL skills in science

The five skill groups and where you practise them.

Approaches to Learning (ATL) skills are the "how to learn" skills. You use all five groups in science lessons, practicals and examinations.

Skill groupWhat it looks like in sciencePractise it on this site
🧠 ThinkingWorking out why a result is odd and changing the method; reading a graph you have never seen; linking ideas from Biology, Chemistry and PhysicsStep 5 (Challenge) of every lesson
🔎 ResearchTelling a reliable source from a biased one, checking a statistic, and listing your sources honestlyCriterion D questions
💬 CommunicationTurning data into a clear graph, a diagram into labelled parts, and an idea into a short paragraph with the right wordsThe graph, drawing and table tools inside questions
🗓 Self-managementBreaking a long investigation or a revision plan into small daily steps; staying calm in a testMy learning plan on your dashboard
🤝 SocialSharing roles in a practical (timing, measuring, recording), checking each other's readings, keeping everyone safeGroup practicals and Live Check with your teacher

A quick self-check

  • Can I explain today's idea to someone else in three sentences?
  • Did I check a second source before I trusted a fact?
  • Does my graph have a title, labelled axes and units?
  • What will I do first, next and last for my next task?
  • Did everyone in my group have a job?
