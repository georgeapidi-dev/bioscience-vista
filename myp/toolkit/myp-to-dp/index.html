MYP toolkit

🎓 From MYP 5 to the Diploma

What changes, what to practise, and SL or HL.

After MYP 5 you can study a Diploma science: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Environmental Systems and Societies, or Sports, Exercise and Health Science. Here is what changes.

MYP sciencesDiploma sciences
Marked onFour criteria (A to D), levels 1 to 8A final grade from 1 to 7
ExaminationsA two-hour on-screen examinationWritten papers worth 80%: Paper 1A (multiple choice), Paper 1B (data-based questions), Paper 2 (longer answers)
Your own investigationCriterion B and C reportsThe scientific investigation, worth 20%: your own research question, up to 3,000 words

Skills to build now

  • Mathematics. Go beyond means: learn standard deviation, percentage uncertainty and how uncertainties combine.
  • Graphs. Plot error bars and learn what a statistical test tells you.
  • Writing. Practise explaining with precise terms: Diploma mark schemes reward exact wording.

Standard Level or Higher Level?

LevelTeaching timeChoose it if
Standard Level (SL)150 hoursYou enjoy the science, or need a balanced load beside other demanding subjects
Higher Level (HL)240 hoursYou want more depth and more mathematics, or plan to study medicine, engineering, pharmacy or a science at university
