🖥 The on-screen examination

The MYP 5 examination: tasks, marks, topics and practice.

In MYP 5 (Grade 10) schools can enter students for an on-screen examination in Biology, Chemistry, Physics or Integrated sciences. It lasts two hours and is taken on a computer.

TaskCriteriaMarks
Knowing and understandingA25
Investigation skillsB and C50
Applying scienceD25
Whole examinationA, B, C and D100

Tasks and marks are from the current MYP sciences subject brief on the IB website.

Topics the examination can draw on

The current IB brief lists these topics for the sciences examinations: atomic structure and bonding · cells · cycles · electromagnetism · evolution · interactions between organisms · forces · states and properties of matter · metabolism · organisms · waves. The lessons on this site cover them subject by subject:

SubjectTopics
🌿 BiologyCells · organisms · processes (photosynthesis and respiration) · metabolism · evolution · interactions with the environment · interactions between organisms · human interactions with environments · biotechnology
⚗️ ChemistryPeriodic table · matter · pure and impure substances · bonding · types of chemical reaction · naming and classifying organic compounds · the atmosphere
⚡ PhysicsForces and energy · heat, light and sound · waves · electromagnetism · atomic physics · astrophysics
🔗 Integrated sciencesAtoms · bonding · cells · electromagnetism · forces and energy · fuels · interactions between organisms · matter · metabolism · periodic table · systems

How to prepare

  1. Work through your subject with Pathway 2: browse by subject, from Level 1 to Level 5.
  2. Do the exam-style questions after each lesson. Each set says which criterion it practises.
  3. Practise planning an investigation and evaluating data: half the marks are for criteria B and C.
  4. Sit a full practice examination under timed conditions.
