💡 What is a course? A course is a whole level or grade, with every subject EduVista has for it:

  • IGCSE (Cambridge or Edexcel): all the sciences and mathematics
  • AS & A Level (Cambridge or Edexcel): all the sciences and mathematics
  • IB Diploma: all the sciences, mathematics, computer science and digital society
  • IB MYP, Cambridge Lower Secondary and Kenya CBE: one grade or stage, for example Grade 7, with all its sciences and mathematics

You pay one price for each course you choose. You choose 1, 4 or 12 months when you pay: 4 months cost 3½ months, and 12 months cost 10 months. Prices are in Kenyan shillings (KES).
