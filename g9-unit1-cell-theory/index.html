STEP 2 · EXPLORE

How did the first cell come about?

25 – 35 min · Activity 2

🎬 Videos for this lesson

▶ The mysterious origins of life on Earth (TED-Ed)Discuss with your group first, then watch, then answer. Do not write while the video is playing.
Hypotheses, not facts. Nobody was there, and nobody can re-run the experiment. These are hypotheses supported by different evidence. Some scientists think more than one is partly true
HypothesisThe ideaEvidence for it
Primordial soupEarly Earth had no oxygen. Simple gases were hit by lightning and UV light and reacted to form simple organic molecules such as amino acids, which built up over millions of years.In 1953 Miller and Urey mixed those gases in a flask, passed sparks through it, and produced amino acids.
Hydrothermal ventsLife began in hot, mineral-rich water rising from cracks in the deep-sea floor. Tiny holes in the rock acted like the first compartments; the chemicals provided energy.Deep-sea vents today are full of life that needs no sunlight. The rock forms natural cell-sized pockets.
RNA worldBefore DNA and proteins there was RNA, which can both carry information and speed up reactions, so it could copy itself.Some RNA molecules found today, called ribozymes, really do act as catalysts.
PanspermiaThe building blocks of life, or life itself, arrived from space on meteorites and comets.Meteorites such as the Murchison meteorite contain amino acids that formed in space.

Figure 3 · From chemicals to the first cell: the order scientists suggest

  • 1 Simple gases and water on early Earth (no oxygen), hit by lightning and UV light
  • 2 Simple organic molecules form, such as amino acids
  • 3 These join into larger molecules: proteins and nucleic acids
  • 4 Fatty molecules form spheres in water: the first membranes (protocells)
  • 5 A self-copying molecule, probably RNA, appears inside
  • 6 The first simple cells: prokaryotes, about 3.5–3.8 billion years ago
  • 7 Some prokaryotes begin photosynthesis and release oxygen
  • 8 The first complex cells: eukaryotes, about 2 billion years ago

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Cell theory: how we found out cells exist

40 – 55 min · Activity 3
▶ The wacky history of cell theory (TED-Ed)Watch, read the summary below, then share with your group before you write.
YearScientistWhat they did
1590sZacharias and Hans JanssenBuilt one of the first compound microscopes: two lenses instead of one.
1665Robert HookeLooked at thin cork, saw tiny box-like compartments, and called them cells after the small rooms monks lived in. He was seeing the empty walls of dead plant cells.
1674Anton van LeeuwenhoekGround much better lenses; first to see living single-celled organisms ("animalcules"), bacteria and sperm cells.
1831Robert BrownIdentified the nucleus inside plant cells.
1838Matthias SchleidenConcluded that all plants are made of cells.
1839Theodor SchwannConcluded that all animals are made of cells, and so all living things are.
1855Rudolf VirchowArgued that every cell comes from another cell that already existed.
The cell theory: copy these exactly. 1. All living things are made of one or more cells. 2. The cell is the basic unit of structure and function in living things. 3. All cells come from cells that already exist. A theory is not a guess: it has been tested many times and never shown to be wrong
