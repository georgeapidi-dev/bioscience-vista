How BioscienceVista works

BioscienceVista is an online science classroom for IB MYP, IB Diploma, Cambridge IGCSE and A Level. You learn with interactive lessons and virtual labs, practise with questions that are marked straight away, and see your progress and your teacher's feedback in one place.

✨ Create a free account🔑 Log in📚 Browse the catalogue🗂 Find a page

Getting started

Five steps

  1. 1
    Create your account

    Register as a student, parent or teacher. Students can learn on their own or join a class.

  2. 2
    Join your class (optional)

    Type the class code your teacher gives you. Your teacher approves you and can then set work and see your progress.

  3. 3
    Learn

    Open a course or the Learning centre. Every interactive lesson follows six inquiry steps: Question → Explore → Test → Explain → Challenge → Reflect, with read-aloud, pictures, simulations and a timer.

  4. 4
    Practise and take assessments

    Run virtual labs, play games, revise with flashcards, and take question sets and timed examinations your teacher sets.

  5. 5
    Check your feedback

    Your dashboard shows every mark, what you did well, what to improve and what to study next. Your teacher can add comments.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is BioscienceVista free?

Creating a student account is free. Free accounts open the free lessons, labs and tools; Premium opens every course. Students in a teacher's class get the work that teacher sets.

What is a class code?

A short code your teacher gives you. Type it when you register (or later on your dashboard) to join the class. Your teacher approves you, then sees your work and can send you feedback.

Who can see my marks and answers?

Only you, your teachers, your parents (if they link with your parent code) and the site admin. Nothing you hand in is public.

How is my work marked?

Choice, matching, labelling and calculation questions are marked straight away. Written answers are checked against a mark scheme; your teacher confirms the mark and can add comments. Some answers also get AI suggestions to help you improve, which your teacher can review.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Every page works on phones, tablets and computers, and you can install it as an app. Many pages also work offline once opened.

Which courses are there?

Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Environmental Systems and Societies, Mathematics for science, and MYP Science for Grades 6 to 10, for IB MYP, IB Diploma, Cambridge IGCSE and A Level.

I am stuck. Where do I get help?

Ask the Help Desk any science question, post in the forum, or write to your teacher in Messages. Inside a lesson, press Stuck? for a hint.

Is AI allowed?

Follow your school's academic integrity rules. The AI here gives feedback and hints; it does not do the work for you, and everything you hand in must be your own.

Ready to explore?

Open a lesson, run a virtual experiment, or ask a question. Your learning is saved for you and your teacher.
