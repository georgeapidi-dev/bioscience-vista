5.3 Sustainable development · Lesson 3 (HL): Managing pollution and waste: monitoring, prevention and reduction of pollution and waste using digital systems

IB Diploma Digital Society · 5.3 Sustainable development · Lesson 3 · 5.3C · HL only (AHL) · 90 minutes · Prepared by VistaEd
Guiding question: Can digital systems help monitor, prevent and reduce pollution and waste?
Virtual labsGraph and drawing tools
🎯 Start here

Low-cost air-quality sensors in cities such as Nairobi, Delhi and Kampala reveal pollution that official monitors miss. Satellites spot methane leaks from oil and gas sites. Smart bins and AI sorting robots improve recycling. An augmented reality app shows the hidden waste behind everyday purchases. Digital systems can make pollution visible, and making it visible is the first step to reducing it.

STEP 1 · QUESTION

Monitoring pollution

0 – 10 min · 5.3C
  • Low-cost sensors measure particulate matter (PM2.5) and gases; dense networks reveal local hotspots.
  • Citizen science: residents host sensors and share data.
  • Satellites: detect methane leaks, oil spills, gas flaring, large floating plastic, deforestation fires.
  • Environmental justice: poorer neighbourhoods often face more pollution but have fewer official monitors.

STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Prevention

10 – 25 min · 5.3C
  • Design software to reduce material use and toxic substances.
  • Predictive maintenance: sensors detect equipment problems before leaks or spills.
  • Satellite and drone monitoring enforce regulations, deterring illegal dumping and flaring.
  • Data on product lifecycles inform bans and standards (e.g. on single-use plastics).
✏️ Worked example: Case: an AR app showing hidden waste
  1. A digital artist creates an AR app visualising the waste implications of common purchases (an IB guide sample).
  2. Pointing a phone at a product shows its packaging, transport and end-of-life waste.
  3. Aim: change buying behaviour and promote circular economy principles.
  4. Question: does awareness change behaviour, and is the data accurate?

Reduction

25 – 40 min · 5.3C

  • AI and robotic sorting improve recycling quality and speed.
  • Deposit-return schemes with digital tracking raise bottle and can return rates.
  • E-waste take-back schemes and apps finding repair or recycling points.
  • Behaviour change apps and feedback (e.g. household waste dashboards).
