By the end of this lesson I will be able to

  • Describe the levels of organisation: cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism.
  • Give examples of tissues, organs and organ systems in plants and animals.
  • Describe diffusion as the way substances move into and out of cells.
  • Cell → tissue (similar cells) → organ (several tissues) → organ system → organism.
  • Animal examples: muscle tissue, stomach, digestive system.
  • Plant examples: xylem tissue, leaf, shoot system.
  • Diffusion: net movement of particles from high to low concentration.
  • Oxygen diffuses into cells; carbon dioxide diffuses out.
  • Faster when warmer, with a bigger difference in concentration, or over a shorter distance.
The big ideaCells form tissues, tissues form organs, organs form organ systems, and systems work together in an organism.

STEP 1 · QUESTION

Levels of organisation

0 – 10 min · Criterion A
  • Cell: basic unit (muscle cell).
  • Tissue: a group of similar cells doing the same job (muscle tissue).
  • Organ: different tissues working together (heart, stomach, leaf).
  • Organ system: organs working together (circulatory system). Organism: a whole living thing.

STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Organ systems

10 – 25 min · Criterion A
  • Digestive: breaks down food (stomach, intestines, liver).
  • Circulatory: transports substances (heart, blood vessels, blood).
  • Respiratory: gas exchange (lungs, trachea).
  • Nervous: coordination (brain, spinal cord, nerves). Plants have organs too: roots, stems, leaves, flowers.
✏️ Worked example: Levels of organisation

  4. Organ system: digestive system → organism.

STEP 3 · TEST

Diffusion

25 – 40 min · Criteria A and C
  • Diffusion is the net movement of particles from an area of high concentration to an area of low concentration.
  • Oxygen diffuses from the lungs into the blood, and from the blood into cells; carbon dioxide diffuses the other way.
  • Diffusion is faster when the temperature is higher, the concentration difference is larger and the surface area is bigger.

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Explain: working together

40 – 52 min · Criterion A
  • Systems depend on each other: digested food is carried by the circulatory system; oxygen from the respiratory system is carried by blood to every cell.
  • If one system fails, others are affected.
