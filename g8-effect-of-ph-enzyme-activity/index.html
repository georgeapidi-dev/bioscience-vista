Notes

pH is a number from 0 to 14 that shows how acidic or alkaline a solution is. It depends on how many hydrogen ions (H⁺) the solution contains. Today everything stays at room temperature; you change only the pH.

The practical: five buffers, three repeats

📺 Watch and learn

How temperature and pH affect enzymesCognito · 3:37 · plays here
  • Label five tubes pH 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11. Put 5 cm³ of the matching buffer into each.
  • Put THREE potato discs of the same size into each tube. Do not choose the biggest ones.
  • Add 10 cm³ of hydrogen peroxide and start the stopwatch. After exactly 60 s, measure the height of the foam in mm.
  • Repeat for all five pH values, twice more, so you have three readings at each pH.
