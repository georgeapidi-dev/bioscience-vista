Vista Education (VistaEd)

Privacy policy

How VistaEd (biosciencevista.com and the VistaEd app) collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Who we are

VistaEd (Vista Education) is an online learning platform created and run by George Apidi, a science teacher. The website biosciencevista.com and the VistaEd app for phones, tablets and computers are the same service. For any privacy question, use Contact us.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, account type (student, teacher or parent), school name, country, and the courses and level you choose.
  • Learning records: your answers in lessons, quizzes and examinations, marks, feedback, time spent on tasks, attendance in live lessons, forum posts and messages you send.
  • Class information: the classes you join or teach, and what teachers set and mark.
  • Payments: if you buy Premium, the plan, amount and date. Card payments are handled by Paystack and M-Pesa payments by Safaricom; we never see or store card numbers or PINs.
  • Use of the site: sign-ins, pages opened and the device type, used to keep accounts secure and to improve the site.

How we use it

  • to give you your lessons, marks, feedback and dashboard, and to let your teacher (and, for students, a linked parent) see your progress;
  • to send emails about your account, your classes, reminders about tasks, your free trial and Premium;
  • to give detailed feedback and tutoring on written answers: the question, the mark scheme and your answer are sent to an AI service for marking and hints (no other personal details are sent);
  • to keep the site secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage;
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the website pages;
  • Resend: sending emails;
  • Paystack and Safaricom M-Pesa: payments;
  • Groq and Anthropic: AI feedback and tutoring;
  • Daily.co: video for live lessons;
  • Google Fonts: text fonts on the pages.

Each provider only receives what it needs to do its job.

Who can see your information

You can see your own work and results. Your teachers see the work and results of the classes you are in. A parent linked to a student can see that student's progress. The site admin can see accounts to run and support the site. Other learners cannot see your marks.

Children

Many VistaEd learners are school students. Students under 18 should use VistaEd with the knowledge of a parent or their school. Schools and teachers who add students are responsible for having permission to do so. A parent can ask us to see or delete their child's information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open, so that your work and results are there when you come back. When an account is deleted, its personal details and learning records are deleted, except payment records that must be kept for accounting.

Your choices

  • see and change your details on your Account page;
  • turn off the weekly summary email on your Account page;
  • ask for a copy of your information or for corrections through Contact us;
  • delete your account: see Delete your account.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Access to every record is checked by the database itself, so each person only reaches what they are allowed to see. Passwords are stored by the sign-in service and are never visible to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and important changes are announced on the site.
