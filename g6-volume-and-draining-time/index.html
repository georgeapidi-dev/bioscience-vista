STEP 3 · TEST

Apparatus and method

15 – 30 min · Q6–Q7
  • 1. Clamp the burette in the retort stand. Check it is straight up.
  • 2. Put a beaker under the tap.
  • 3. Use the funnel to fill the burette with coloured water. Take the funnel out.
  • 4. Open the tap for one second to push out any air bubble in the tip. Close it.
  • 5. Set the water to 10 cm³. Read the BOTTOM of the meniscus at eye level.
  • 6. Check the tap is fully closed.
  • 7. Open the tap FULLY and start the stopwatch at the same moment.
  • 8. Stop the stopwatch at your agreed stopping point: the moment the water stops running as a stream.
  • 9. Write the time to one decimal place.
  • 10. Do the same volume two more times, then 20, 30, 40 and 50 cm³.

Notes

Mean time = (Trial 1 + Trial 2 + Trial 3) ÷ 3. Example: 25.1 + 25.8 + 25.0 = 75.9, and 75.9 ÷ 3 = 25.3 s. Write the mean to one decimal place, with the unit. If one trial is very different from the other two (an anomalous result), do that trial again and use the new time.
