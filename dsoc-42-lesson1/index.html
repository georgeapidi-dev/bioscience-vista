STEP 1 · QUESTION

Digital business

0 – 10 min · 4.2A
  • Digital systems in business: cloud software, data analytics, online marketing and sales, digital payments, supply-chain tracking, video meetings.
  • Small firms reach global customers through platforms and messaging apps.
  • A few large technology platform companies gain great market power through data and network effects.

STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Diversity in businesses

10 – 25 min · 4.2A
  • Digital tools can widen participation: remote work for disabled people and carers, translation tools, accessible software, global hiring.
  • But the technology industry itself is often not diverse; AI hiring tools can reproduce bias; online harassment drives some groups away.
✏️ Worked example: Case: a microworker's day
  1. A university graduate in Kenya or the Philippines logs on to a microwork platform.
  2. Tasks: labelling images for AI, transcribing audio, checking content.
  3. Pay per task can be a few cents; work comes and goes unpredictably.
  4. This hidden labour helps train AI systems used worldwide.

STEP 3 · TEST

Working practices

25 – 40 min · 4.2B
  • Office design: open-plan, hot-desking, hybrid offices with video rooms.
  • Remote working: grew rapidly during the COVID-19 pandemic; many firms now use hybrid arrangements.
  • Digital nomads: people who work online while travelling; several countries now offer special visas for them.
  • Employee monitoring: software tracking keystrokes, screens or location.
  • Employee organisations: unions use apps and social media to organise, including gig workers.

Your answers

Q3. Evaluate remote working from the perspectives of employees and employers. [3]
Support hintEmployees' gains and losses, then employers.
This step: 3 marks
STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Gig economy, crowd work and microwork

40 – 55 min · 4.2B
  • Gig economy: short-term tasks paid per job through apps (ride-hailing, food delivery, cleaning).
  • Crowd work / microwork: online tasks split into small pieces done by many people worldwide (data labelling, content moderation, surveys).
  • Algorithmic management: apps assign work, set pay and rate workers.
  • Debate: are gig workers independent contractors or employees? Courts and laws differ by country.

Your answers

Q4. Discuss whether gig work benefits workers. [4]
Support hintBenefits, insecurity, power, judgement.
This step: 4 marks
STEP 5 · CHALLENGE

Automation and employment

55 – 70 min · 4.2B
  • Automation affects tasks more than whole jobs: routine physical and cognitive tasks are most exposed; generative AI now affects writing, coding and design tasks too.
  • New jobs appear (data, AI maintenance, care, green energy); others change.
  • Responses: lifelong learning, retraining, social protection.
