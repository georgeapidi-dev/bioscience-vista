STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Malnutrition

35 – 50 min · Task 3 · watch and write
  • MALNUTRITION means a diet that is not right: too little of a nutrient (undernutrition), or too much or unbalanced food (overnutrition, for example obesity).
  • Undernutrition can cause stunted growth, weak immunity and anaemia (too little iron), and rickets (too little vitamin D).
  • Overnutrition can lead to obesity and related illnesses, such as heart disease and type 2 diabetes.
