Notes

enzyme concentration means how much enzyme there is in a volume. More enzyme means more ACTIVE SITES, so more substrate molecules can be worked on at once. Double the enzyme and you roughly double the rate, while there is plenty of substrate. The enzyme is a catalyst, so the same enzyme molecule works on substrate over and over.

Notes

at first, more substrate speeds the reaction up, because empty active sites are found more often. Then every active site is full: the graph goes FLAT, the PLATEAU. The enzymes are not getting tired and are not being used up. They are all busy. From the plateau, the only way to speed up is to add MORE ENZYME.

Notes

biological washing powder contains protease (protein stains, such as blood and egg) and lipase (greasy stains). Enzymes work at low temperatures, so a 40 °C wash saves electricity. Wash too hot and the enzymes denature. Industry likes enzymes because they are specific and work at low temperature and normal pressure, which is cheap and safe.
