How temperature and pH affect enzymesCognito · 3:37 · YouTube
  • As temperature rises from cold, the enzyme works faster: the molecules move faster and collide more often.
  • The OPTIMUM temperature is where the enzyme works fastest, about 37 to 40 °C for most human enzymes.
  • Above the optimum, the shape of the active site changes. The enzyme is DENATURED, permanently.

STEP 3 · TEST

pH and concentration

30 – 40 min · Q3 · explain (30–40 min)
  • pH works in the same way as temperature: every enzyme has an optimum pH.
  • MORE ENZYME means more active sites, so the rate goes up.
  • MORE SUBSTRATE speeds the reaction up at first, but then the graph goes flat (the plateau) because every active site is busy.

STEP 6 · CHALLENGE

Enzymes at home and at work

70 min · Q7 and Q8 · evaluate (70 min)
  • Biological washing powder contains protease and lipase, which break down protein and fat stains.
  • Enzymes work at low temperatures, so washing at 40 °C saves electricity. Wash too hot and the enzymes denature.
  • Industry uses enzymes because they are specific and work at low temperatures and normal pressure.
