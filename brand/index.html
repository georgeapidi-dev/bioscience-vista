The Learnova look

How the site looks and sounds, so every page, lesson and worksheet feels like one place. Designed for Learnova.

Logo

A leaf-and-lens mark: living things seen up close. Use it on a light or dark background with clear space around it equal to the height of the leaf. Do not stretch or recolour it.

Outfit for headings

Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. It was designed for low-vision readers, so letters such as I, l and 1 are easy to tell apart.

Voice

  • Talk to the student: "you", short sentences, plain words.
  • Say what to do next, not only what went wrong.
  • Feedback names the science: "name the monomer" beats "add more detail".
  • Credit images and sources.

Learning design

Every interactive lesson follows six inquiry steps (question, explore, test, explain, challenge, reflect) and Universal Design for Learning: several ways in (text, images, simulations), several ways to show learning, and instant feedback.
