Biography

I am an international science educator specialising in IB, IGCSE and A Level Biology and Chemistry, committed to inquiry-driven, student-centred learning that develops curiosity, critical thinking and scientific understanding. I have taught in international schools in Kenya and Ethiopia since 2019, and I now teach DP Biology and MYP Science at Aga Khan Academy Mombasa.

My practice is grounded in concept-driven pedagogy with a strong emphasis on investigation and real-world application. I am highly experienced in supporting students through the IB Internal Assessment, from topic selection and experimental design to data analysis and report writing, and I mentor Extended Essays and support TOK-related inquiry.

Beyond the classroom I contribute to curriculum planning, assessment design, STEM leadership and pastoral care, and I use ManageBac, Google Classroom, PhET and Gizmos to make learning engaging and feedback timely. I am committed to developing scientifically literate, curious and independent learners who can apply science to real-world challenges.
