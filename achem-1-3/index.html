STEP 1 · QUESTION

Where are the electrons?

0 – 8 min · 1.3.1 – 1.3.2
  • Electrons occupy shells (energy levels) numbered by the principal quantum number, n = 1, 2, 3, 4 … The higher the value of n, the higher the energy and the further the shell is from the nucleus.
  • The ground state of an atom is the arrangement of its electrons with the lowest possible energy.

STEP 2 · EXPLORE

Shells, sub-shells and orbitals

8 – 20 min · 1.3.3 – 1.3.4, 1.3.9
  • Each shell is divided into sub-shells: n = 1 has 1s; n = 2 has 2s and 2p; n = 3 has 3s, 3p and 3d; n = 4 has 4s, 4p, 4d and 4f.
  • An orbital is a region of space around the nucleus that can hold up to two electrons with opposite spins.
  • An s sub-shell has 1 orbital (2 electrons), a p sub-shell has 3 orbitals (6 electrons) and a d sub-shell has 5 orbitals (10 electrons).
  • Shapes: an s orbital is spherical. A p orbital is dumb-bell shaped; the three p orbitals in a sub-shell lie at right angles to each other.

STEP 3 · TEST

Filling order and electronic configurations

20 – 34 min · 1.3.5 – 1.3.7
  • Sub-shells fill in order of increasing energy: 1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p.
  • Within a sub-shell, electrons occupy the orbitals singly first, with parallel spins, and only then pair up. This keeps repulsion between electrons as small as possible.
  • Full configuration of iron (Z = 26): 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ 3d⁶ 4s². Shorthand, using the previous noble gas: [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s².
  • In electrons-in-boxes notation each box is one orbital and each arrow is one electron; two arrows in a box point in opposite directions (opposite spins).

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

Electrons in boxes

34 – 48 min · 1.3.7
  • In electrons-in-boxes notation each box is one orbital and each arrow is one electron. Two arrows in the same box point in opposite directions (opposite spins).
  • Electrons in the same sub-shell occupy separate orbitals first, with parallel spins. The single arrows are the unpaired electrons.
  • Example: oxygen, 1s² 2s² 2p⁴, has one pair and two unpaired electrons in its three 2p orbitals.
