By the end of this lesson I will be able to

  • Identify the main nutrients in food.
  • Classify foods by their main nutrients.
  • Explain what a balanced diet means.
  • Connect food and nutrition to your own culture and everyday life.
The big ideaFoods are mixtures of nutrients. Find the nutrients first, then judge whether the meal is balanced.

STEP 4 · EXPLAIN

What is a balanced diet?

45 – 55 min · Explanation (15 min)
  • A balanced diet contains all the nutrients, water and fibre the body needs, in the right amounts.
  • No single food gives everything, so variety matters.
