  • 🔒 Enzyme activity laboratory
    Molecular biology · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Change temperature, pH, substrate, enzyme concentration and inhibitors. Measure initial rates, find the optimum and compare inhibitors.
  • 🔒 Osmosis laboratory: plant and animal cells
    Biology · MYP 3–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Put potato cells and red blood cells in sucrose solutions. Track water potential, turgor, plasmolysis and haemolysis; find the isotonic point.
  • 🔒 DNA replication laboratory
    Molecular biology · MYP 5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Use helicase, primase, DNA polymerases and ligase to copy DNA, build leading and lagging strands, then measure how proofreading cuts errors.
  • 🔒 Heart and circulation laboratory
    Human physiology · MYP 3–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Watch the cardiac cycle with live pressure traces and valves. Change heart rate, contractility and valve disease; measure stroke volume and cardiac output.
  • 🔒 Nerve impulse laboratory: neural signalling
    Human physiology · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Stimulate an axon and watch Na⁺ and K⁺ channels open. Find the threshold and refractory period, and measure how diameter, myelin, temperature and drugs change impulse speed.
  • 🔒 Chemical signalling laboratory: ligands and receptors
    Molecular biology · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Add adrenaline, insulin or oestradiol to target cells. Watch transmembrane and intracellular receptors, second messengers and amplification; plot dose–response curves and test a blocker.
  • 🔒 Miller–Urey laboratory: the origin of organic molecules
    Biology · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · A Level — Run Miller and Urey's 1953 spark experiment: boil water, spark reducing, neutral or oxidising gases, watch the trap turn red-brown and identify amino acids by paper chromatography.
  • 🔒 Properties of water laboratory: hydrogen bonds in action
    Biology · MYP 3–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Animations of polarity and hydrogen bonds, cohesion and surface tension, adhesion and capillary action, heat capacity, water as a solvent and floating ice. Measure capillary rise and heating.
  • 🔒 Photosynthetic pigments laboratory: chromatography and Rf values
    Biology · IB DP · A Level — Separate leaf pigments on a TLC plate or paper, watch the solvent front rise, measure each band and calculate Rf values to identify carotene, chlorophylls and xanthophylls.
  • 🔒 Photosynthesis laboratory: limiting factors and the action spectrum
    Biology · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Count oxygen bubbles from pondweed as you move the lamp, change CO₂ and temperature, and use coloured filters. Find the limiting factor and build an action spectrum.
  • 🔒 Cell respiration laboratory: the respirometer
    Biology · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Germinating seeds take in oxygen and the coloured liquid moves. Change temperature, seeds and mass; calculate oxygen uptake and the rate of respiration.
  • 🔒 Tropisms laboratory: phototropism, gravitropism and auxin
    Biology · MYP 3–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Light seedlings from one side, lay them down or spin them on a clinostat. Remove or cover the tip, add mica or auxin agar; watch auxin move and measure the angle of curvature.
  • 🔒 Microscope laboratory: mounts, measuring cells, mitotic index and stomata
    Biology · MYP 3–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Make temporary mounts, stain and focus; measure cells with an eyepiece graticule; count mitosis in root tips and tumours, stomata in leaf casts; view arteries, veins, stems and roots.
  • 🔒 Ecological sampling laboratory: quadrats, Lincoln index and chi-squared
    Environmental science · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Place random quadrats in a field of daisies, mark and recapture beetles, and test two plant species for association with a chi-squared test.
  • 🔒 Transect laboratory: distribution and range of tolerance
    Environmental science · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Walk a belt transect from woodland into a meadow, measure light and soil moisture, record plant cover and correlate distribution with an abiotic factor.
  • 🔒 Population growth laboratory: yeast and duckweed
    Environmental science · IB DP · A Level — Grow yeast (haemocytometer counts) or duckweed; change food, temperature and starting number; plot exponential and sigmoid growth on normal and log scales.
  • 🔒 Mesocosm laboratory: what keeps an ecosystem stable?
    Environmental science · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · A Level — Seal pondweed, snails and decomposers in a jar; change light and numbers; track oxygen, carbon dioxide and populations for 12 weeks.
  • 🔒 Natural and sexual selection laboratory: Endler's guppies
    Biology · IB DP · A Level — Model Endler's guppy ponds: change the predator and the gravel, switch female choice on or off, and track the mean number of spots over generations.
  • 🔒 Lung volumes laboratory: spirometry
    Human physiology · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Breathe into a spirometer; read tidal volume, IRV, ERV and vital capacity from the trace; compare people and the effect of exercise.
  • 🔒 Pulse rate laboratory: exercise and recovery
    Human physiology · MYP 3–5 · IB DP · IGCSE — Feel the radial or carotid pulse or use a pulse oximeter; measure heart rate at rest and in recovery after exercise for fitter and less fit volunteers.
  • 🔒 Range of motion laboratory: measuring joint angles
    Human physiology · IB DP · A Level — Use a goniometer to measure the range of motion of the elbow, knee, shoulder and hip in different planes, and compare people.
  • 🔒 PCR and gel electrophoresis laboratory
    Molecular biology · IB DP · A Level — Run a thermocycler, then a gel with a DNA ladder; solve a forensic case, a paternity test and a virus test.
  • 🔒 Genetic crosses laboratory: dihybrid ratios and chi-squared
    Biology · IB DP · A Level — Count purple, yellow, smooth and wrinkled kernels on maize cobs and test the 9:3:3:1 and 1:1:1:1 ratios with chi-squared; discover linked genes.
  • 🔒 Acid–base titration laboratory
    Chemistry · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Titrate strong and weak acids and bases with indicators and a pH meter. Plot the pH curve, find the equivalence point, pKa and an unknown concentration.
  • 🔒 Rates of reaction laboratory: marble chips and acid
    Chemistry · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Collect carbon dioxide in a gas syringe while you change acid concentration, chip size and temperature. Find initial rates, the order of reaction and explain with collision theory.
  • 🔒 Electrolysis laboratory: products and Faraday's law
    Chemistry · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Watch ions move to the electrodes in copper sulfate, brine, dilute acid and silver nitrate. Weigh the electrodes, collect gases, write half-equations and find the Faraday constant.
  • 🔒 Chemical equilibrium laboratory: Le Châtelier's principle
    Chemistry · MYP 5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Heat, cool and compress a sealed syringe of brown NO₂ and colourless N₂O₄. Watch the colour shift, calculate Kc and decide whether the reaction is endothermic.
  • 🔒 Enthalpy of combustion laboratory: burning alcohols
    Chemistry · MYP 4–5 · IB DP · IGCSE · A Level — Burn methanol to pentan-1-ol under a copper can of water. Use Q = mcΔT to find enthalpies of combustion, the trend per CH₂ group and how much heat is lost.
